Delete Your Duly Account

Last updated: 7 October 2026

You can delete your Duly account and its data at any time, either in the app or by emailing us. Duly is developed by Dikshanta Ghimire.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Duly and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Data → Data Deletion.
  3. Tap Delete account and type the confirmation shown.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email hisabkitab.team@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Duly account, with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we will complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • Your profile, including your plan and subscription records.
  • All bills you have saved, including notes.
  • Your payment history.
  • Your Smart Add usage records.

This is permanent and cannot be undone. Copies held in our service providers' routine backups are removed as those backups expire.

What is not deleted automatically

  • Premium subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions to stop future charges.
  • Data on your phone: settings and scheduled reminders stored on your device are removed when you uninstall Duly.
  • Backups you exported: any backup files you saved or shared are under your control and are not deleted by us.
  • Google Play records: Google keeps its own records of purchases, under Google's privacy policy.
