Duly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Duly is a bill reminder app that helps you track recurring bills and avoid missed payments. This policy explains what information Duly collects, why, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible for your data

Duly is developed and operated by Dikshanta Ghimire, based in London, United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at hisabkitab.team@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and how you signed in (email and password, or Google), and when you accepted our Terms of Use.
  • Bill information you enter: bill names, amounts, due dates, how often they repeat, and any notes.
  • Payment history: a record of when you mark a bill as paid and the amount.
  • Smart Add text: if you use Smart Add, the short text you type to describe a bill (for example "Gym 40 on the 30th").
  • Subscription details: if you buy Premium, which plan you bought, its status and when it renews. Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card details.
  • Usage limits: a daily count of Smart Add requests, used to prevent abuse.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes or hits an error, technical details such as the error message, app version, device model and operating system version. Crash reports do not include your email address, IP address or screenshots.

Information that stays on your device

Some information is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us: your reminder schedule and reminder settings, payday and weekend buffer settings, and app lock settings. If you set a PIN, only a scrambled (hashed) version is stored on your device. Fingerprint and face unlock are handled entirely by your phone's operating system. Duly never receives your biometric data.

How we use your information

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store your bills and payment history and sync them to your account.
  • To show what is due, flag bills at risk, and power the app's features.
  • To turn Smart Add text into a bill.
  • To provide Premium features you have paid for.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.

Our legal reasons for using your data

  • Contract: running your account, storing your bills, reminders and Smart Add, and providing Premium.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping Duly secure, preventing abuse, and finding and fixing crashes.
  • Legal obligation: keeping records of purchases and of your acceptance of our Terms where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show ads, we do not use your data for advertising or tracking, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

AI and your data

We do not use your bills, payment history, Smart Add text or any other personal data to train AI models, and we do not allow our providers to do so.

Smart Add first tries to understand your text on your device. If it cannot, the text is sent through our server to OpenAI, which reads it and returns the bill name, amount, due date, and frequency. Only the Smart Add text is sent, not your other bills or your account details. OpenAI does not train on data sent through its API, and we ask it not to store the request. Please avoid typing sensitive personal information into Smart Add.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run Duly. They process data only on our instructions and only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: account sign-in, database, and server hosting.
  • Google: Google Sign-In (if you choose it) and Google Play billing.
  • OpenAI: processing Smart Add text, as described above.
  • Sentry: receiving crash reports so we can find and fix bugs.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

International transfers

Some of our providers may process data outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or standard contractual clauses.

Cookies and tracking

The Duly app and this website do not use cookies, advertising IDs, analytics or tracking tools, so there is nothing to accept or reject. The app only stores what it needs on your device to keep you signed in and remember your settings.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, bills, payment history, and Smart Add usage records are permanently deleted. Copies held in our providers' routine backups are removed as those backups expire. Crash reports are deleted automatically by Sentry after its retention period. See our data deletion page for details.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data (you can edit your bills in the app at any time);
  • delete your data (you can delete your account in the app);
  • receive a copy of your data (use Settings → Your data → Export backup);
  • object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email above. We will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each account can only access its own data. You can also protect the app with a PIN or biometric lock. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Duly is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app.

Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, email hisabkitab.team@gmail.com.
